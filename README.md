💰 SmartBudget Manager

SmartBudget Manager is an intuitive, full-featured personal finance and expense tracking application designed to help individuals and households take control of their money. With real-time expense categorization, customizable budget limits, visual spending analytics, and smart savings goals, managing your finances has never been easier.

✨ Features

📊 Interactive Dashboard: Get a bird's-eye view of your total balance, monthly income, total expenses, and remaining budget at a glance.

🏷️ Smart Categorization: Automatically or manually categorize transactions (e.g., Groceries, Rent, Utilities, Entertainment, Dining, Transport).

🎯 Custom Budget Limits: Set monthly spending caps per category and receive visual warnings when you approach or exceed your limits.

📈 Visual Analytics & Reports: Interactive pie charts, bar graphs, and trend lines to analyze spending habits over weeks, months, or years.

🔄 Recurring Transactions: Automate regular bills, subscriptions, and salary deposits so you never miss an entry.

🏦 Savings Goals: Create dedicated savings buckets (e.g., Emergency Fund, Vacation, New Car) and track your progress toward each target.

💱 Multi-Currency Support: Track accounts and expenses in multiple currencies with customizable formatting.

📤 Data Import & Export: Export your financial reports to CSV or PDF for tax preparation or offline record-keeping.

🔒 Secure & Private: Built with authentication and encrypted data storage to keep your financial information safe.
